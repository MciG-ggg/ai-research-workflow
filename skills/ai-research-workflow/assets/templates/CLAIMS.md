## Evidence Requirements

- Every non-draft claim must link to `RESULTS.md`, run summaries, metrics files, figures, source citations, or reproducibility evidence.
- `supported` requires direct evidence and matching evaluation scope.
- `contradicted` and `inconclusive` results must be preserved; do not delete or hide negative results.
- Claims must not generalize beyond the dataset, benchmark, seed count, model family, compute budget, or implementation quality that was actually tested.
